Monthly Energy Usage

  • July: Issue. Focusable custom object lacks programmatic role and accessible name.
  • August: Issue. Named as an image despite being interactive; interaction/state is not communicated.
  • September/November: No issue for this test. Interactive role, meaningful name, and state are exposed.

Workspace Floor Plan

  • 4A: Issue. Focusable custom marker lacks role and accessible name.
  • 4B: Issue. Identity and option role are exposed, but selection state is omitted.
  • 4C/4D: No issue for this test. Relevant identification and state are programmatically exposed.

Comfort Preference

  • Cool: Issue. Focusable custom control lacks programmatic role.
  • Mild: Issue. Slider semantics omit current value.
  • Balanced/Warm: No issue for this test. Role, name, range, current value, and value text are exposed.

Why the catch-all applies

These examples use custom chart objects, spatial markers, and a custom preference scale rather than conventional accordions, show/hide controls, checkboxes, radio groups, links, or ordinary buttons.
